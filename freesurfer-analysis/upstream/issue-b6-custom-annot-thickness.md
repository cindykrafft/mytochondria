Title: mris_anatomical_stats per-ROI thickness includes zero-thickness
medial-wall vertices for annotations not constrained by cortex.label

**Summary.** The per-ROI thickness accumulation in mris_anatomical_stats
filters only on the annotation index (`if (v0->marked < 0) continue`,
mris_anatomical_stats.cpp) and never consults `?h.cortex.label`, while the
same program's whole-hemisphere MeanThickness (with -cortex) and the TH3
GrayVol are cortex-masked. Vertices that carry an ROI label but lie outside
the cortex label have thickness exactly 0 (pial pinned to white at the
medial wall) and are averaged into ThickAvg / inflate ThickStd.

For the standard aparc/a2009s pipeline this cannot fire: `mris_ca_label -l
cortex.label` relabels every non-cortex vertex to unknown, so standard
annotations nest inside the cortex label (verified on the bert test
subject: zero labeled vertices outside cortex.label; our reimplementation
matches the shipped reference aparc table on all 34 ROIs to <= 0.0005 mm).

It does fire for **custom parcellations transferred by registration**
(`mri_surf2surf --sval-annot` from fsaverage - Schaefer, HCP-MMP, Yeo -
then mris_anatomical_stats): mapping the published Schaefer2018-200
fsaverage annotation to bert biases 9/100 left-hemisphere parcels; the
worst, a cingulate parcel over the callosum, reports 2.309 mm vs 2.556 mm
cortex-masked (-0.247 mm), with 35 of its 359 vertices at thickness 0.
Since the annot/cortex-label mismatch varies with anatomy (and atrophy),
this is a confound in exactly the limbic/cingulate parcels those atlases
are used to study.

**Ask:** either (a) apply the -cortex label to the per-ROI thickness
accumulation when given (arguably the expected semantics; NumVert/area
could keep full counts), or (b) exclude thickness==0 vertices from ROI
thickness means, or at minimum (c) document that per-ROI stats are not
cortex-masked and recommend intersecting custom annots with cortex.label.
