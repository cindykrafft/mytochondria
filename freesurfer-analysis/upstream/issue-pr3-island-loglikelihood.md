Title: GCSAreclassifyLabel compares Gibbs log-likelihoods as ints —
annotation islands absorbed by adjacency order, not likelihood

**Summary.** In `utils/gcsa.cpp`, `GCSAreclassifyLabel` (called by
`GCSArelabelIslands`, which runs in every `mris_ca_label` postprocess)
declares `int ... max_ll, ll` and stores the return of
`gcsaNbhdGibbsLogLikelihood` — a double — into them before comparing
(dev/v7.4.1: gcsa.cpp:1557,1581; v6.0.0: gcsa.c:1589). Adjacent labels'
log-likelihoods typically differ by well under 1.0 exactly at the ambiguous
boundaries where island relabeling happens, so the int truncation destroys
the comparison and the island is absorbed into whichever neighboring label
appears first in the vertex adjacency list rather than the
maximum-likelihood one.

The sibling function `GCSAreclassifyMarked` already declares
`double ll, max_ll` (gcsa.cpp:1303), so this looks like an oversight rather
than a design choice.

**Impact.** Small disconnected annotation islands (<10% of the label's main
component) are reassigned quasi-arbitrarily but deterministically per mesh;
across subjects this biases area/thickness statistics of small parcels
(e.g. bankssts, transversetemporal, parsopercularis at 3-way junctions).

**Affected versions:** v6.0.0 through dev.

A fix is available: https://github.com/freesurfer/freesurfer/compare/dev...cindykrafft:freesurfer:fix/gcsa-island-loglikelihood
