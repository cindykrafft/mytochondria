TITLE: replaceOutliers: round replacement counts instead of truncating

Closes #NNN (companion issue).

`as.integer()` truncates toward zero, so every replacement count produced by
`replaceOutliers()` was biased low by 0.5 on average — a trimmed-mean
prediction of 3.9 became 3. This wraps both branches in `round()` before the
integer coercion.

Effect: outputs change only for analyses where Cook's-triggered outlier
replacement actually ran (default `minReplicatesForReplace=7`), and only in the
final integer digit of the replaced counts; downstream statistics shift
accordingly (typically imperceptibly at bulk counts, visibly at low counts).
Existing regression references that cover replacement will need the
corresponding update — the diff is the fix working.

Validation (shipped v1.42.1, 7v7 with injected outliers, 600 replaced
entries): mean bias of replacement counts vs `trimBaseMean * sf` is **-0.513**
as shipped and **+0.002** with `round()`; 51.5% of replaced integer values
differ. Script: `audits/deseq2/verify/dsn2_replace_rounding.R` in
cindykrafft/research-software-audit.
