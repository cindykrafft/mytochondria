TITLE: Documentation request: MACS p-values are P(X > t), one probability atom below the standard discrete p-value P(X >= t)

**Summary — not a regression report.** Every MACS version since 1.4 (2008)
computes the per-position score as the strictly-greater Poisson tail:
`get_pscore` calls `poisson_cdf(n, lam, lower=False)` and all upper-tail
implementations sum from k+1 (verified in 1.4.3 `lib/Prob.py`, 2.1.1 and
2.2.7.1 `Prob.pyx`, and current `MACS3/Signal/Prob.py`). So the reported
p-value is P(X > t), where the standard definition of a discrete p-value —
"probability, under the null, of a result at least as extreme as observed" —
is P(X ≥ t).

The two differ by the probability atom at t, and at peak-calling counts that
atom is not small. At the default `-log10 p = 5` cutoff (exact Poisson, per
position; script `tail_convention.py` in
https://github.com/cindykrafft/mytochondria/tree/main/audits/macs2/verify):

| local lambda | boundary count t* | P(X ≥ t*) | P(X > t*) (MACS) | admitted null rate vs P(X ≥ t) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 0.5 | 6 | 1.4e-05 | 1.0e-06 | 14.1× |
| 1 | 8 | 1.0e-05 | 1.1e-06 | 9.1× |
| 2 | 10 | 4.7e-05 | 8.3e-06 | 5.6× |
| 5 | 17 | 2.0e-05 | 5.4e-06 | 3.7× |
| 10 | 26 | 1.8e-05 | 6.4e-06 | 2.8× |
| 25 | 49 | 1.4e-05 | 7.0e-06 | 2.0× |

The low-λ rows are exactly the sparse-background regime of ATAC-seq and
CUT&RUN — the majority of current MACS use. Q-values inherit the convention
through the p→q table.

**Why file this as documentation rather than a behavior change:** 17 years of
published peak sets are internally consistent under the current convention,
and switching would silently change every user's peak lists. What seems worth
having:

1. a sentence in the docs/FAQ stating the convention explicitly ("the reported
   p-value is P(count > observed); at a given cutoff this is more permissive
   than P(count ≥ observed) by the probability mass at the observed count");
2. (optional) a `P(X ≥ t)` mode or a note in the paper-citing docs, so users
   comparing MACS p-values to other Poisson-based tools understand the offset.

Happy to send a docs PR for (1) if the framing is agreeable.

Found during a source audit of MACS against 475 papers using it in six
high-impact journals (2021–2026).
