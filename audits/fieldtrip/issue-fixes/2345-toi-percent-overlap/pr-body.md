Title: ft_freqanalysis: a percentage in cfg.toi is the overlap between windows, not the step (fixes #2345)

## Summary

Fixes #2345.

**How to reproduce:** with `cfg.method = 'mtmconvol'` and 2 s of data, `cfg.t_ftimwin = 0.5`, compare `cfg.toi = '10%'` and `cfg.toi = '90%'`. The help says the percentage "specifies the degree of overlap between the shortest time windows from cfg.t_ftimwin", so `'90%'` should give the denser time axis. On master it is the other way round: `'10%'` gives 41 time points 50 ms apart (90 % overlap) and `'90%'` gives 5 time points 500 ms apart (no overlap at all). Only `'50%'` behaves as documented, because it is the one value for which step and overlap coincide. A minimal script and its output before and after are in the linked project directory.

**What was the reason?** In `ft_freqanalysis.m` the percentage was used directly as the step between consecutive time points, `linspace(begtim, endtim, round((endtim-begtim)./(overlap*min(cfg.t_ftimwin)))+1)`, i.e. the code implemented "percent step" while the help and the comment say "percent overlap" (this dates from #193, 2016).

**What is the solution?** The step is now the non-overlapping part of the shortest window, `(1-overlap)*min(cfg.t_ftimwin)`, which is what the help describes and what @NirOfir proposed in the issue. A percentage of 100 or more (a step of zero or less) now gives an `ft_error` instead of an attempt to allocate an infinite time axis. `'50%'` is unchanged, so existing analyses with the value used in the tutorials are not affected; other percentages change (a user who wrote `'90%'` and got 10 % overlap will now get 90 %).

**Test:** `test/test_issue2345.m` (`DATA no`, synthetic data) checks for 10/25/50/75/90 % that the number of time points is the one implied by the documented meaning, that more overlap gives more time points, that `'50%'` still gives 17 points, and that `'100%'` is an error. It fails on master (`cfg.toi = '10%' gave 81 time points, expected 10`) and passes with this change.

**What was run:** `test_issue2345`, plus the 14 data-free `test/test_*.m` scripts that exercise `mtmconvol`, on unpatched master and on this branch under GNU Octave 8.4 (MATLAB is not available to me). The 7 that pass on master still pass; the 7 that fail do so identically on both trees for Octave reasons (no `dpss`, no `taylorwin`, MATLAB-only `strncmp`/`sq_string` behaviour).
