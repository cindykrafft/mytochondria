### PR 1 — `fix/per-sequence-quality-mean-rounding` — "Round the per-read mean quality instead of truncating it"

Fixes #NNN.

`PerSequenceQualityScores.processSequence` summed the quality characters into an `int` and divided by the read length with integer division, so a read whose mean quality is 27.9 was counted at 27. The "Per sequence quality scores" histogram, the most frequent mean quality that the warn/fail thresholds test, and MultiQC's copy of the histogram sat up to one unit (0.5 on average) below the true per-read means.

This sums into a `long` and rounds the mean to the nearest integer. On 2,000 reads with an exact mean of per-read means of 30.02 the histogram mean goes from 29.53 to 30.02, and every bin equals the number of reads whose rounded mean is that value. Nothing else changes.
