### PR 1 — `fix/undefined-strand-transcripts` — "Treat transcripts with undefined GTF strand ('.') as '+' in Aligned.toTranscriptome.out.bam and count them from both strands in STARsolo"

Fixes #NNN.

**Purpose: bug fix.** A transcript whose GTF strand is `.` is stored with strand 0, and five
places test `trStr==1 ? Str : 1-Str` (or the `geneFull.str` equivalent), which treats strand 0
like `-`:

- `Transcriptome_quantAlign.cpp` — every record of such a transcript in
  `Aligned.toTranscriptome.out.bam` had its strand flag inverted and its sequence
  reverse-complemented while the position was that of the `+` strand (the coordinate
  conversion in `alignToTranscript` only runs for strand 2). RSEM/salmon then see a read that
  mismatches the transcript at every base.
- `Transcriptome_classifyAlign.cpp` (Gene), `Transcriptome_geneFullAlignOverlap.cpp`
  (GeneFull), `Transcriptome_geneFullAlignOverlap_ExonOverIntron.cpp`,
  `Transcriptome_alignExonOverlap.cpp` (GeneFull_Ex50pAS) — STARsolo with `--soloStrand
  Forward` dropped every sense read of a `.` gene and counted them with `Reverse` instead
  (GeneFull_Ex50pAS counted none with either).

**Expected change in behaviour.** Only transcripts and genes whose GTF strand is `.` are
affected. Their transcriptome records are now written like those of `+` transcripts
(flag and sequence consistent with the position), and STARsolo `Forward`/`Reverse` counting
accepts reads from both strands for them — the convention `--quantMode GeneCounts` already
applies ("genes w/o strand will accept reads from both strands",
`Transcriptome_geneCountsAddAlign.cpp`). **Default behaviour for `+`/`-` transcripts does
not change**: on a 16-transcript synthetic annotation with 300 single-end and 200 paired-end
reads per transcript, every transcriptome record of the 14 stranded transcripts and every
`ReadsPerGene.out.tab` row is identical before and after the change; `--soloStrand
Unstranded` is unchanged.

**Test.** `extras/tests/scripts/testUndefinedStrand.sh [STAR]` (self-contained: 3-kb LCG
genome, a `+` and a `.` copy of the same two-exon structure, eight reads; needs `samtools`
for the BAM check) fails on 2.7.11b and passes with this change.

**Changelog.** `CHANGES.md` entry under `STAR 2.7.11c --- unreleased` (issue number to be
filled in).
