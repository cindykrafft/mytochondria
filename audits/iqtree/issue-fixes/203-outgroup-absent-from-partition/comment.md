The cause is `PhyloTree::setRootNode()` (`tree/phylotree.cpp:486`): with `-o`, it is also called on the per-partition trees of the supertree (from `saveCheckpoint()`/`getTreeString()` during the fast ML tree search, and from `PartitionModel::computeMarginalLhForPartitions()`), and a partition tree does not contain a taxon that has no data in that partition, so `findNodeName()` returns null and `ASSERT(root)` aborts. The same assertion fires for the 4-taxon quartet trees of `-lmap` with `-o` (#89). The fix skips outgroup taxa absent from the tree at hand and roots such a tree at its first taxon, as when no outgroup is given; the outgroup is still validated against the alignment as before.
