Reproduced on master (3.1.3) with a synthetic 4-taxon codon alignment: two different `+FU` vectors give the same log-likelihood as `GY+F`, and the report says `GY+F` with 60 free parameters, exactly as described. The cause is one line in `ModelCodon::init`: a user-defined frequency type is replaced by the model's default for every mechanistic model, and `initGY94()` reports `FREQ_EMPIRICAL` as its default, so `+FU` becomes `+F` for GY-type models. The MG family needs that fallback (to `F3X4`); GY does not.

PR #NNN restricts the fallback to nucleotide-targeted (MG-style) models. With it, `GY+FU` is reported with 0 free parameters and the likelihood matches an independent GY94 computation to all printed digits for both a skewed and a uniform frequency vector.
