### PR 1 — `fix/mapq-coef-fac-float` — "Declare mapQ_coef_fac as float so the MAPQ length factor is ln(50)/ln(l)"

Fixes #NNN.

`mem_opt_init()` and `-Q` assign `log(mapQ_coef_len)` to `mapQ_coef_fac`, but the field is declared `int`, so ln 50 = 3.912 becomes 3 and `mem_approx_mapq_se()` uses `3/ln(l)` instead of `ln(50)/ln(l)` for alignments of 50 bp or longer. The factor drops from 1 at l = 49 to 0.767 at l = 50 and enters squared, so MAPQs below the cap for such alignments are about 41% lower than the formula intends. In the issue's example, a read with a one-mismatch second hit gets MAPQ 27 at 49 bp and 15 at 50 bp.

This declares the field `float`. `mem_opt_t` keeps its size and layout. Only MAPQ changes. On the issue's reads, POS, CIGAR, flags, mate fields, TLEN, NM, MD, AS, XS, SA and XA are identical before and after. The 40-60-bp scan is now continuous (27 up to 52 bp, then 26, 25, 24), and a port of the formula with ln 50 reproduces all 158 single-end MAPQs of the wider test. `make` builds cleanly with the same warnings as `master`.
