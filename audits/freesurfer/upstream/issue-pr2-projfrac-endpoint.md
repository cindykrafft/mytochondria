Title: mri_vol2surf --projfrac-avg silently drops the deepest sample for
common (min,max,delta) values; same in mri_surf2vol --fill-projfrac

**Summary.** The projection loop in `mri_vol2surf.cpp` accumulates a float:
`for (ProjFrac=ProjFracMin; ProjFrac <= ProjFracMax; ProjFrac += ProjFracDelta)`
(v7.4.1 ~line 596; identical in v6.0.0 and dev). Accumulated rounding error
pushes the final value above ProjFracMax by ~1e-7 for many delta values, so
the last sample fails the `<=` test and is silently dropped:

| invocation | samples taken | max depth | effective mean depth |
|---|---|---|---|
| --projfrac-avg 0 1 0.1 (the documented example) | 10 | 0.9 | 0.45 |
| --projfrac-avg 0.2 0.8 0.1 | 6 of 7 | 0.7 | 0.45 |
| --projfrac-avg 0 1 0.05 | 20 | 0.95 | 0.475 |
| --projfrac-avg 0 1 0.2 or 0.25 | all | 1.0 | 0.50 (unaffected) |

(Reproduced by compiling the loop verbatim in a standalone harness; the
per-sample `printf` in mri_vol2surf shows the same.)

**Impact.** The "average across cortical depth" is really an average whose
mean depth sits at 0.45, displaced toward the white surface by
0.05 x thickness at every vertex of every subject — and whether that
happens depends on the delta chosen, so the flag's semantics silently vary
with step size. `--projfrac-max` maxes over the same truncated range, and
`mri_surf2vol --fill-projfrac` (default 0 1 0.05) skips its outermost,
pial-most fill pass.

**Affected versions:** at least v6.0.0 through dev (loop unchanged).

A fix (integer-stepped loop) is available: https://github.com/freesurfer/freesurfer/compare/dev...cindykrafft:freesurfer:fix/vol2surf-projfrac-endpoint
