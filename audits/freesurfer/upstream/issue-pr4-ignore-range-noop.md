Title: mris_anatomical_stats -i <low> <high> is a silent no-op — prints
that it filtered thickness, but never does

**Summary.** The `-i` option parses its arguments and prints "only
considering thicknesses in the range [low,high]"
(mris_anatomical_stats.cpp:1260-1267 in dev), but the filter is never
applied: its only intended consumer, `MRIScomputeCurvatureStats`, is
declared and defined yet never called (verified by grep in v6.0.0, v7.1.1
and dev). ThickAvg, ThickStd and MeanThickness are computed over all
vertices regardless of -i.

**Impact.** Any study that passed `-i` believing thickness outliers (e.g.
zero-thickness frozen vertices, >5mm artifacts) were excluded published
unfiltered statistics while the program stated otherwise. The numbers are
identical to a run without -i.

**Repro.** Run mris_anatomical_stats on any subject with and without
`-i 1 5` and diff the stats output: byte-identical, despite the printed
message.

**Affected versions:** at least v6.0.0 through dev.

A fix that applies the documented range to the thickness statistics (with
default output unchanged) is available: https://github.com/freesurfer/freesurfer/compare/dev...cindykrafft:freesurfer:fix/anatomical-stats-thickness-range
An acceptable alternative would be removing the option; either way the
silent no-op should go.
