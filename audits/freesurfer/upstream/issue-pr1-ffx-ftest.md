Title: GLMtestFFx: multi-row (F) contrast p-values inflated ~J-fold (Wald
statistic passed to the F CDF without dividing by J)

**Summary.** In `utils/fsglm.cpp`, `GLMtestFFx` computes
`gamma' * inv(gCVM) * gamma` and passes it directly to
`sc_cdf_fdist_Q(., J, ffxdof)` (v7.4.1: fsglm.cpp:703-711; same in v6.0.0's
fsglm.c and current dev). That quantity is a Wald statistic, distributed as
`J * F(J, dof)` under the null, so for a J-row contrast the reported
p-values are inflated roughly J-fold. The random-effects path (`GLMtest`)
is correct — it folds J into its denominator (`dtmp = rvar * C[n]->rows`).

**Impact.** Any `mri_glmfit` fixed-effects analysis (`--yffxvar`/`--ffxdof`)
using a multi-row contrast: with J=2, a contrast at true p=0.05 is reported
as p=0.0025. Single-row (t) contrasts are unaffected.

**Evidence.** Monte Carlo calibration (200,000 null draws; 3-group design,
J=2 equality contrast, heteroscedastic known variances as in the FFx
model): null rejection rate at alpha=0.05 is **0.224** with the shipped
code and **0.050** after dividing the statistic by J.

**Affected versions:** v6.0.0 through dev (code unchanged across v6.0.0,
v7.1.1, v7.4.1, dev).

A one-line fix is available: https://github.com/freesurfer/freesurfer/compare/dev...cindykrafft:freesurfer:fix/fsglm-ffx-fstat
