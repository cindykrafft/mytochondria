### PR 1 — `fix/null2-bytrace` — "Fix two errors in the trace-based null2 correction (insert residues; first residue of each sampled domain)"

Fixes #NNN.

Two one-line fixes to the null2 correction used for domains in regions resolved by stochastic traceback clustering:

1. `p7_Null2_ByTrace()` (SSE, NEON, VMX) added every M or I residue to the match cell, so insert residues were scored with match emission odds. They now go to the I cell, which the existing sum already weights with insert odds 1.0, the same as `p7_GNull2_ByTrace()`. A new `utest_null2_trace()` in the SSE null2 test driver compares the two implementations on profile-emitted traces. It fails without this change.
2. `region_trace_ensemble()` gave each sampled domain's first residue (`sqfrom`) the outside value 1.0. The first loop now stops before `sqfrom`.

Effect with default options on human Swiss-Prot and the tutorial models: 17 of 194 fn3 hits and 9 of 483 Pkinase hits move by up to 0.9 bits, and E-values by 0.74–2.0×. Reported and included sets were unchanged in these runs. Details and reproduction are in the issue. `make check` passes (Easel 102/102, HMMER 307/307 exercises). I only built and ran the SSE implementation. The NEON and VMX changes are the same line.
