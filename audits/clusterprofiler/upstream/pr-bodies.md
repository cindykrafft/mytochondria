### PR 1 — `fix/gsea-sign-conditioned-pvalue` — "Condition sample/permute/adaptive GSEA p-values on the sign of the permutation ES"

Closes #NNN

`gsea(method = "sample" | "permute")` and `adaptive = TRUE` divided the number of permutations at
least as extreme as the observed ES by the total number of permutations (`src/gsea.cpp:339,517,536`).
The GSEA nominal p-value (Subramanian et al. 2005), fgsea, DOSE's former permutation path and this
package's own `multilevel` method use the permutations whose ES has the same sign as the observed
one, so these p-values were about half of the multilevel ones for the same gene set (exact
enumeration on 24 genes, sets of 3: 0.45 vs 0.88; median ratio 0.50 over 14 sets).

Changes:

- count the same-sign permutations (`count_same_sign`) and report
  `min(1, (count_better + 1) / (count_same_sign + 1))` in `gsea()` and `gsea_adaptive()`;
- on the `sample` path compare the observed ES computed by `calculate_es_sparse()`, the routine that
  scores the permutations, so a set lying entirely at one end of the list (ES = ±1) is no longer
  reported at the permutation floor;
- `tests/testthat/test-gsea.R`: a test that enumerates the exact null of all C(24, 3) gene sets and
  checks the `sample`, `permute` and adaptive p-values against it (fails on `devel`, passes here);
- `NEWS.md` entry under `# enrichit 0.2.2.001` (please renumber to your next version).

The default `multilevel` method and all ES/NES/leading-edge output are unchanged. Full suite: 37
tests pass.
