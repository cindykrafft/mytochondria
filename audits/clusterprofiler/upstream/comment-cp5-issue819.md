Title: (comment on #819) "Big differences in enricher results between clusterProfiler 4.16.0 and clusterProfiler 4.20.0": the cause is enrichit 0.1.x BH over zero-overlap sets, fixed in 0.2.0

<!-- post only after reading the 6 existing comments (not readable from the audit session); skip if the thread already names enrichit 0.2.0 -->

The drop in significant terms between 4.16 and 4.20 comes from the engine that 4.19.3+ delegates
to, `enrichit`, and it depends on which enrichit version is installed rather than on the
clusterProfiler version:

- enrichit ≤ 0.1.5 (CRAN until 2026-06-30) ran `p.adjust` over every gene set in the
  `minGSSize`–`maxGSSize` window, including sets with no query gene (`Count = 0`), whereas ≤ 4.18
  (DOSE) adjusted only over the sets hit by at least one query gene.
- enrichit 0.2.0 (CRAN 2026-07-01, NEWS: "exclude zero-overlap gene sets from `ora_gson()` before
  multiple-testing correction … #821 & #819") restores the old denominator.

Measured on GO BP (org.Hs.eg.db 3.18.0, 6,379 terms of size 10–500) with a 198-gene query:
enrichit 0.1.4 adjusts over 6,379 terms (2,523 of them with `Count = 0`) and reports 513 terms at
p.adjust ≤ 0.05; enrichit 0.2.1 and DOSE 3.30.0 adjust over the 3,856 hit terms and report 694.
`enricher()` p-values themselves are identical between the engines (and equal to
`phyper(k-1, M, N-M, n, lower.tail = FALSE)` to 1e-15).

So `packageVersion("enrichit")` ≥ 0.2.0 should give the 4.16-style counts again. A 40-line
reproduction with synthetic data (200 sets, 99 of them with `Count = 0` under 0.1.4; p.adjust of the
planted set 5.2e-16 vs 2.6e-16) is here if useful:
https://github.com/cindykrafft/mytochondria/tree/main/audits/clusterprofiler/upstream/mcve_cp5_ora_zero_overlap_bh.R
