# Duplicate check before filing — enrichit CP4 (2026-10-05)

Read-only check; nothing was posted, opened or pushed. Kit base: enrichit `devel` @ `23f64ce` (0.2.5). Fix branch: `fix/gsea-sign-conditioned-pvalue` @ `217a839`.

**Finding:** `gsea(method = "sample" | "permute")` and `adaptive = TRUE` divide by all permutations instead of the same-sign ones, so p ≈ ½ of the GSEA convention. A second, smaller problem is on the `sample` path: for ES = ±1 the dense and sparse ES computations round differently, giving p = 1/(nPerm+1).

**Verdict: DUPLICATE of YuLab-SMU/enrichit#3.** It was opened 2026-10-01 by @musharna (not us) and reports the main CP4 defect. Do not open a new issue. If the owner wants to proceed, the right move is a comment on #3 and/or a PR that says "Closes #3". The comment should add the ES = ±1 dense/sparse sub-problem (not in #3), the exact-enumeration check, and the offer of the ready patch.

### What enrichit #3 covers, read on the public page; 0 comments as of 2026-10-05

- **Covered:**
  - `method = "sample"`, `method = "permute"` and `adaptive = TRUE`.
  - The `src/gsea.cpp` count `pes >= obs_es` divided by `nPerm + 1`.
  - A null simulation: fraction of null sets with p < 0.05 is 0.122 for sample vs 0.044 for multilevel; max p 0.565; median ratio 0.51.
  - The proposed fix: condition the denominator on sign "as fgsea does", `pmin((1+nLeEs)/(1+nLeZero), (1+nGeEs)/(1+nGeZero))`. This is equivalent to our `(count_better+1)/(count_same_sign+1)`.
