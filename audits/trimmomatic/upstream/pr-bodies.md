### PR 1 — `fix/palindrome-mismatch-penalty` — "Charge Q/10 per mismatch in ILLUMINACLIP palindrome mode"

Fixes #NNN.

`calculatePalindromeDifferenceQuality` divided two `int`s (`-qual1 / 10`), so a palindrome
mismatch cost `int(Q/10)`: nothing below Q10, 1 for Q10–Q19, and so on. Simple mode
(`calculateDifferenceQuality`) and the README both use Q/10. This divides by `10.0f` in the
two branches, as simple mode does.

Adds `IlluminaPalindromeMismatchPenaltyTest`: a 2x50 pair with a 42-base insert and two
Q19 errors scores 29.9 under the documented rule and must not be clipped at threshold 30
(it scores 31.7 with the integer penalty and was clipped); the same pair with one Q19
error scores 32.4 and must be clipped. The first test fails on unmodified `main`.

`mvn -B test`: 259 tests pass on `main`, 261 with this change.

### PR 2 — `fix/maxinfo-normalisation` — "Keep MAXINFO score tables inside long range for large target lengths"

Fixes #NNN.

`MaximumInformationTrimmer` scales its two log-score tables to `long` with one ratio and
took the `Math.max` of the two candidate ratios, i.e. the ratio computed for the table with
the smaller maximum. Applied to the other table, it pushes `(long) (array[i] * ratio)` past
`Long.MIN_VALUE` once `targetLength` exceeds ~2461 × strictness (248 at 0.1, 495 at 0.2);
the saturated value then wraps when the quality sum is added, the first base gets the
highest score, and every read is trimmed to one base. From `targetLength` 711 on the
logistic term overflows `Math.exp` and the same happens at every strictness.

This change (a) uses `Math.min`, so both tables stay inside `long` range (the quality sum
over `LONGEST_READ` bases stays below `Long.MAX_VALUE / 2`), (b) seeds `calcNormalization`
with `Math.abs(array[0])`, and (c) evaluates `log(1/(1+e^x))` as `-x` for `x > 700`.
Results at ordinary settings are unchanged: 5,000 reads with Illumina-like qualities
give identical surviving lengths before and after the change under seven typical
`targetLength:strictness` pairs, and the existing MAXINFO tests pass.

Adds `MaximumInformationTrimmerLargeTargetTest` (four tests; three fail on unmodified
`main`).

`mvn -B test`: 259 tests pass on `main`, 263 with this change.
