Title: Fix spectral init being non-deterministic under random_state on a regular graph component

Fixes #1277.

**Cause.** When enough exact copies of one point are present (the issue's duplicated rows), they form their own connected component whose fuzzy graph is complete with every edge weight 1, i.e. a regular graph. `_spectral_layout` (`umap/spectral.py`) starts ARPACK from `v0 = np.ones(n)`; on a regular graph the all-ones vector is an exact eigenvector of the normalised Laplacian, so the Lanczos iteration breaks down at the first step and ARPACK restarts from its own internal random generator, which `random_state` does not control and which is process-wide state. The eigenvectors it returns for that component therefore change from call to call, and the SGD amplifies the different initialisation into embeddings that differ by several units. On a complete graph of 10 identical points, `eigsh(..., v0=np.ones(10))` returns different eigenvectors on every call in the same process; with a seeded random `v0` it returns the same ones. The same start vector also makes `eigsh` fail outright on some regular graphs (a random 4-regular graph here), which triggers the "Spectral initialisation failed" fallback.

**Fix.** Keep the all-ones start everywhere it works; only when every vertex has the same degree (`np.allclose(sqrt_deg, sqrt_deg[0])`) draw `v0` from the seeded generator instead. Seeded results on graphs that are not regular are unchanged, and the check reuses the degree vector the function already computes.

**Tests.** `test_spectral_layout_regular_graph_is_deterministic` (`umap/tests/test_spectral.py`): `spectral_layout` on the complete graph of twelve duplicates, three calls with `random_state=42` must agree. `test_repeated_points_reproducible_with_random_state` (`umap/tests/test_umap_repeated_data.py`): 200 random points plus sixteen copies of one far-away point, two seeded fits must be identical. Both fail before this change (the layouts and the embeddings differ) and pass after it.

**Run.** `pytest umap/tests/test_spectral.py umap/tests/test_umap_repeated_data.py umap/tests/test_umap_ops.py`: 32 passed, 1 skipped on master; 34 passed, 1 skipped (126 s) with this change. `black --check` leaves `umap/spectral.py` and `umap/tests/test_spectral.py` unchanged; `test_umap_repeated_data.py` has a pre-existing double blank line after its imports that black 26 would collapse, left as it is on master. Minimal reproduction (synthetic data with the issue's shape: 1000 unique rows + 200 repeated 4x, `metric="cosine"`, `n_components=5`, `random_state=42`): three seeded fits differ by up to 7.6 before, identical after; sixteen copies of one point: differ by up to 17.5 before, identical after.
