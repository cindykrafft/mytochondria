The non-determinism is in the spectral initialisation, not in the nearest-neighbour search: enough copies of one point form their own connected component whose fuzzy graph is complete with every edge weight 1 (a regular graph), and on a regular graph the all-ones start vector that `_spectral_layout` hands to `eigsh` is an exact eigenvector of the normalised Laplacian, so ARPACK's Lanczos iteration breaks down immediately and restarts from its own internal random generator, which `random_state` does not control.
