Thanks, that makes sense. I hadn't thought of TLEN 0 as meaning "unknown".

I've reworked the second commit that way (not pushed yet). Pairs in the isize 0 bin are left out of the denominator of the average and of the SD, and the SD loop starts at 1 again as on develop, so they're out of the numerators too. On the test file (10 pairs, two with TLEN 0, the rest 300–360) this gives an average of 323.8 and an SD of 18.0, the values for the eight pairs with a known insert size; develop reports 259.0 and 60.1. `make test` passes.

- the `IS` histogram still has its isize 0 row, and the inward/outward/other pair counts still include those pairs.
